Where each problem stands

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • Parser (#1): fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Closed.
  • Hint (#2): won’t fix, because the booking-v3 form was retired. Closed as not planned. No new reports can come through that form.
  • One-day move (#3): not in the engineering update. The parser fix does not touch it, so it stays open. A report about it is not a duplicate of #4471.

How each report is placed

  1. One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so the same report can appear more than once. Repeats are dropped before anything is counted.
  2. By tenant, not by intake tag. A day-first tenant’s reports through the app, email, or a booking-v2 form went through the shared parser. Form reports from a booking-v3 tenant came through the MM/DD/YYYY hint (causes.ts). The intake tag comes from the reporter’s browser, which is often not the tenant’s locale, so intake often picks the wrong problem: see intake agreed.
  3. One-day moves are their own problem too. In some reports the date moves by one day (Tuesday shows as Monday, one meeting on two days in two time zones). A day/month swap moves a date by months, and no locale setting moves it by one day. The desk listed these reports in desk-notes.json. They stay filed and counted under their tenant’s cause, as the desk counts them, and they also have their own ticket.

I read the bodies to check this. Every body that gives both dates is an exact day/month swap, apart from the one-day moves. The few bodies that give no dates (a request for a “day first” setting, a client who now gives dates in words, October bookings that came through for January) are filed under their tenant’s cause.

What changes for the desk

  • Closing #4471 did not close every problem in it. Close each ticket when its own fix ships.
  • The hint problem lived in the booking-v3 form, not in the parser. The parser fix left it unchanged. Retiring the form ended it.
  • The one-day problem is about time zones. It moves the date by a day, and the day/month fixes do not touch it.
  • Subject-line dedupe put all three problems into one ticket. Until decision 0001 is replaced, check a merged ticket’s bodies before you mark it known.
